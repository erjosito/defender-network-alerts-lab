4. Network-Layer Alerts Status

As of the last poll (10:56 UTC, ~2 hours post-attack), no network-layer alerts (SSH_Incoming_BF, RDP_Incoming_BF, PortScanning, DDOS) have been generated. This is consistent with Microsoft's documentation stating that network-layer detection uses traffic flow sampling with a 1-4 hour detection window.

Network-layer alerts are probabilistic — they detect patterns in sampled traffic flows at the Azure fabric level, not at the VM/OS level. Low-volume attacks or attacks during periods of low sampling coverage may not trigger alerts.
